Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11-11-2025

NYSC MCQ Exam App (“the App”, “we”, “our” or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, NYSC ID, email, and authentication details.
  • Exam & Usage Data: Exam attempts, results, and time spent.
  • Device Information: Device type, system version, and activity logs for exam integrity monitoring.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To verify identity and provide secure exam access.
  • To simulate real NYSC exam conditions and track performance progress.
  • To detect and prevent cheating or exam rule violations.
  • To improve app performance and reliability.

3. Data Security

Your data is protected using encryption and industry-standard security practices. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

4. Offline Support

Exam data may temporarily store offline and sync automatically once online.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use secure third-party services for authentication, hosting, or analytics. These services are required to protect user data.

6. Exam Integrity Monitoring

The App may detect app-switching or suspicious behavior during exams. Violations may result in automatic submission or restrictions.

7. Your Rights

  • Request access to your stored personal information.
  • Request corrections or deletion (where allowed under NYSC policy requirements).

8. Children’s Privacy

This App is intended only for NYSC-registered students and not for children under 13.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when necessary. Continued use of the App means acceptance of the revised policy.
